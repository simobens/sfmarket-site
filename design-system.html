4. Shape, spacing & elevation

The original theme rounded every action to a 30px pill (buttons, inputs, pagination). Cards use a soft 10px radius; the 28×2px accent underline marks section titles.

--sf-radius-pill 30px — buttons & fields
--sf-radius-card 10px — cards
50% — avatars & icons
--sf-shadow-soft — resting cards
--sf-shadow-card — hover lift

Spacing scale: 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 100 px (--sf-space-1…6) · container 1200px · header height 74px.
