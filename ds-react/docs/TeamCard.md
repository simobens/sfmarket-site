Team member card with a gradient initials avatar (avatars alternate teal/accent automatically by grid position), name, and muted caps role. Lay out in a `TeamGrid`.

```tsx
<TeamGrid>
  <TeamCard initials="MB" name="Med Ben Ali" role="General Manager" />
  <TeamCard initials="BA" name="Badr Abari" role="Senior Designer" />
</TeamGrid>
```
